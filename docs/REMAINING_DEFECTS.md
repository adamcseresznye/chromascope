limitations, not a claim of formal validation. See [performance evidence](PERFORMANCE_VALIDATION.md)
and [validation history](VALIDATION_REPORT.md). P1 means high priority before advertising
the affected capability; P2 means a substantive limitation; P3 means follow-up evidence.
